# Product

LendLoop helps neighbors lend or rent everyday items. Phase 1 establishes a polished public entry point and society-first onboarding. A user can eventually be both lender and borrower. The intended roles are `user` and LendLoop-managed `admin`; Trusted Lender and Trusted Borrower are independent badges assigned by LendLoop.

Planned lending flow: browse by society, optionally expand to 500 m or 1 km, request dates, lender approves or declines, participants agree, then private handover details become available. Paid requests may include one borrower offer below the listing price. The lender can accept, counter strictly between offer and list price, or keep the list price. The borrower accepts that final price or walks away. No further bargaining. This flow is **not implemented** in Phase 1.

Future marketing ideas such as society-app partnerships and competitor comparisons are concepts only. There are no claimed integrations, usage statistics, testimonials, or verified residency checks in the current product.

## Implemented in Phase 2

A signed-in, onboarded neighbor can list a tool, preview it, add up to five photos, capture with a camera, manage photo order and cover, edit, unlist, relist or archive. Explore defaults to My Society with 500 m and 1 km scopes, text/category/free-paid filters and sorting. Detail pages show full listing information and a responsive gallery. Distance comes from society centers and is labeled approximate. Active means listed for discovery, not date-checked availability. Request, approval, negotiation, loan, payment and handover flows remain future work.
